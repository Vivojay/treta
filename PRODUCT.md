# Treta product

Treta is for autonomy platform engineers, fleet operators, safety reviewers, simulator engineers, and incident investigators who need a vendor-neutral way to test and supervise heterogeneous autonomous fleets.

The MVP thesis is that a deterministic simulation-first control plane can make missions, safety decisions, failures, and incident evidence explainable before any physical integration is attempted. Success means the same scenario seed produces the same evidence, unsafe assignments are rejected with reasons, and a vehicle-local fallback remains independent of cloud connectivity.

The MVP excludes physical-drone control, customer delivery operations, payments, defence, weapons, facial recognition, public surveillance, and claims of certification or airworthiness. Commercial evolution may add paid provider adapters, larger simulations, enterprise identity, regional control planes, and independently reviewed physical pilots after explicit safety and regulatory gates.
