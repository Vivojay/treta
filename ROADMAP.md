# Treta roadmap

1. **Current:** Phase 0 foundation plus the deterministic end-to-end synthetic delivery/failure/replay slice (`DESIGN.md` §§41-43).
2. **Next:** Durable Firestore/R2-compatible adapters, Firebase emulator authorization rules, fuller command/outbox recovery, and expanded scenario validation.
3. **Then:** Map-focused frontend, MQTT/Mosquitto and NATS adapters, observability profiles, and network fault tests.
4. **Later:** PX4/ArduPilot SITL adapters and documented production-reference infrastructure. Physical-pilot remains disabled pending independent safety, regulatory, insurance, hardware, and operational approval.
